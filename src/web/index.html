KobeOS

Soko Huru keeps making the vacancy posters. KobeOS turns each one into a swipeable job profile and generates a private, real-time recruitment page for the employer client behind it.

The workflow

  1. An employer sends a vacancy to Soko Huru by WhatsApp, email, phone or printed advert.
  2. Soko Huru creates its normal AJIRA EXCLUSIVE poster and publishes it as it always has.
  3. Soko Huru uploads the same poster or message into KobeOS, and Kobe AI reads the details out of it.
  4. Staff check the extraction and press Publish. Two pages are generated at once.
  5. Paid applicants swipe the job card. A right swipe attaches their best CV and files the application.
  6. The employer's page shows the candidate immediately and tracks them to hire or rejection.
