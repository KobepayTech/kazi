KobeOS

The recruitment agency keeps making the posters it already makes. KobeOS turns each one into a swipe-to-apply job card and gives the employer behind it a private page that fills up in real time.

The loop

  1. The agency uploads an existing job poster or pastes the message.
  2. Kobe AI reads the vacancy out of it; staff check the fields beside the original poster.
  3. Publishing creates the swipe card and, for a new client, the employer's private link.
  4. A paid applicant swipes right and confirms; KobeOS attaches the CV it wrote for them.
  5. The employer sees the candidate immediately and shortlists, interviews, hires or rejects.
  6. The applicant's status updates the moment the employer acts.
